## Uniform motion: moving at a constant speed in a straight line

**Outcome SCI10-FM2:** Investigate and represent the motion of objects that travel at a constant speed in a straight line.

### Key words

- **Distance** (d): how far an object travels, no matter which way. A scalar, measured in metres (m).
- **Displacement** (Δd): the change in position, with a direction. A vector, for example 120 m [E].
- **Speed** (v): distance travelled per unit of time. A scalar, in metres per second (m/s).
- **Velocity** (v⃗): displacement per unit of time, with a direction, for example 25 m/s [N].
- **Uniform motion**: motion at a constant velocity: the same speed, in the same direction, all the time.

### The equation

Average velocity = displacement ÷ time interval, or **v = Δd ÷ Δt**.

Rearranged: **Δd = v × Δt** and **Δt = Δd ÷ v**.

Units: keep SI units. Change km/h to m/s by dividing by 3.6 (90 km/h ÷ 3.6 = 25 m/s).

### Worked example 1: a grain truck

A grain truck drives 150 m along a straight grid road in 6.0 s at a steady speed. What is its speed?

v = Δd ÷ Δt = 150 m ÷ 6.0 s = **25 m/s**

### Worked example 2: Highway 1

A car keeps a steady 100 km/h on Highway 1 between Regina and Moose Jaw, 72 km apart. How long does the trip take?

Δt = Δd ÷ v = 72 km ÷ 100 km/h = **0.72 h**, about **43 minutes**.

### Position–time graphs

- On a position–time (d–t) graph, uniform motion is a **straight line**.
- The **slope** of the line is the velocity: slope = rise ÷ run = Δd ÷ Δt.
- A steeper line means a greater speed.
- A horizontal line means the object is **at rest** (velocity = 0).
- A line sloping down means the object is moving back toward the starting point (negative velocity).

### Velocity–time graphs

- On a velocity–time (v–t) graph, uniform motion is a **horizontal line**.
- The **area under the line** is the displacement: area = v × Δt.

### Common mistakes

- Mixing km/h and m/s in one calculation.
- Reading the slope of a d–t graph as the distance travelled.
- Forgetting that a horizontal d–t line means "stopped", not "moving at a constant speed".
- Leaving out the direction when a question asks for velocity or displacement.

### Lab: the constant-velocity car

Students time a battery-powered toy car over 0.5 m, 1.0 m, 1.5 m and 2.0 m, plot position against time, and find the car's speed from the slope of the line of best fit. A typical car gives a slope of about 0.40 m/s.

### Exit question

A cyclist rides 3.0 km along the Wascana trail at a constant 5.0 m/s. How many minutes does the ride take?
(Answer: 3000 m ÷ 5.0 m/s = 600 s = **10 minutes**.)
