# Background photo

`oak-library-1920.webp`, `oak-library-2560.webp` and `oak-library-blur.webp` are resized and compressed copies of one photo:

- **Photo:** "A library themed cafe with wooden shelves" by **Raymond Yeung** on Unsplash
- **Source:** https://unsplash.com/photos/a-library-themed-cafe-with-wooden-shelves-NtCXVLvJfdQ
- **Licence:** Unsplash License (https://unsplash.com/license): free to use, copy and modify, no permission or attribution required. Credit is given here with thanks.

`oak-library-blur.webp` is a small, pre-blurred copy. Funza uses it for the frosted glass effect instead of a live `backdrop-filter`, which is slow on school Chromebooks.
